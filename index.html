04  /  The Document Archive

Permanent. Verified. Uncensorable.

At the core of $THELIST is a permanent on-chain archive of publicly released Epstein-related legal documents. Each document is represented by its SHA-256 hash, registered on Solana with a timestamp, and mirrored to IPFS for decentralised hosting. No government, no platform, no institution can remove this record.

Every document in the archive is sourced exclusively from official public records — court dockets, congressional filings, and government FOIA releases. We make no original allegations. We publish no private information. We simply ensure that what the courts have already made public cannot be quietly forgotten.
